Full sweep — Fri Nov 6, 2026. All 44 venue calendars re-scraped and every row replaced, plus the first Instagram pass since August. 1234 shows across 41 rooms, Nov 6 through Dec 2.

Instagram is back. The endpoints this calendar used to read stories and posts were shut off in the autumn. It now reads the same queries the Instagram website itself uses, which means recent posts and story link stickers are both searchable again — and crucially, without marking anyone’s story as seen. 119 accounts with live stories and 66 profiles were swept; 18 shows came out of it, under the Community / DIY badge.

What the community rows found. Rooms no venue calendar covers: Barzakh Cafe in Crown Heights, Come To Life in East Williamsburg, Cinema Village, National Sawdust, Birdland and Smoke. Where a show turned out to already be on a tracked venue’s calendar, the duplicate was dropped rather than listed twice.

Smalls, Mezzrow and Jazzcultural have stopped publishing prices. Until mid-October their event pages carried an advance and a door figure; both are now gone from the site, and the Ticketing page lists none either. Their 385 rows therefore read “no price published” — the venue does still say a music charge is added to your bar tab, plus one drink per person per set, and that wording is carried through. Nothing was estimated to replace the missing numbers.

Where a price is missing, the venue publishes none. Nothing is estimated. DICE and TicketWeb figures are all-in totals with fees and are labelled as such; Elsewhere’s are Eventbrite starting prices with fees added at checkout. Village Vanguard sells through Squadup, behind bot protection, so its rows read “at door”. Close Up publishes no figure at all. The Jazz Gallery, Ornithology, Five Spot, Jupiter Disco and Red Pavilion publish nothing. Young Ethel’s rests on the venue’s blanket “free every day” claim, not per-event confirmation.

Gaps found and fixed, and one left open. doNYC’s pagination silently skips dates, and this week it hid Nov 15–20 at Cafe Wha?, Nov 11–21 at Purgatory, Nov 13–24 at C’mon Everybody and scattered dates at TV Eye and Littlefield. All of those were refilled from the venues’ own pages, Songkick or BrooklynVegan. Still open: Cafe Wha? Nov 16, Nov 24 and Nov 30 – Dec 1 could not be recovered from any source. Nothing was invented to fill them.

How far ahead each room publishes. Bar LunÀtico’s own site stops at Nov 17 (later dates come from jazzin.nyc and carry no price); Public Records’ DICE page returns only 30 events and stops at Nov 19; Jupiter Disco’s only reachable mirror ends Nov 27; Cassette’s ends Nov 24 and lists bands only, so its DJ and drag nights are probably missing. The Lot Radio publishes the current day and nothing else. Barbès has still not posted a November PDF — all 25 of its rows are residency projections, not confirmed bookings, and are labelled. 92NY yielded one date.

Four rooms returned nothing. Reforesters Laboratory lists no upcoming events at all on Resident Advisor this month. 360 Record Shop, Doublé and Mezcaleria La Milagrosa publish no dated schedule anywhere reachable. None were projected.
